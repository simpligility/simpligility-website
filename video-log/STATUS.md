## 1. Year blocks — resolved, do not reopen

Done on 2026-10-09. Every Sonatype video that is still watchable had its
recording date read off the date shown on screen in the screen share, and each
was split into a dated entry in its own year block. This created new `2016` and
`2015` headings, with both navigation lists updated to match. The *Easy
publishing to the Central Repository* series dissolved entirely into eight dated
entries across December 2015 and January 2016; one video was pulled from *Tips
from the trenches* (Using Docker Repositories with Nexus 3, September 2015) and
one from *Empowering releases with the Nexus Staging Suite* (Module 16, June
2016).

The four remaining series &mdash; *Tips from the trenches*, *Empowering releases
with the Nexus Staging Suite*, *The Nexus 2 minute challenge*, and *Nexus
Professional and Smart Proxy* &mdash; stay grouped under the `2010&ndash;2016`
heading on purpose. Their videos are gone from YouTube, so there is no screen
share to read and no upload metadata to recover, and each series spans several
years. Dating the roughly sixty remaining videos would mean inventing dates for
a factual log. We did what the real evidence allows; going further is a waste of
time. Do not re-propose per-video dating for these.

The known video channels and playlists have been swept and nothing new was
found; what was checked, and the standing "do not re-propose" guards, live in
the skill under **Channels already swept**. There is no harvesting backlog: add
new standalone videos as they appear, following the skill.
